About Us

The vision behind Ephemeral

Ephemeral is a project developed just for fun and experimentation by Priyanshu Kumar — a tech creator, developer, and student at IIT Madras, pursuing a B.S. in Data Science and Applications.

The idea behind Ephemeral is to explore a different approach to online communication, where conversations and media can be temporary rather than permanently stored.

This project is an independent experiment built to learn, create, and explore what’s possible with modern web technologies.
